How it works

Set up in minutes. No sign-up.

There's no email, phone number or password. Your identity is created on your phone, and your recovery phrase is the only key.

  1. Create your family

    Pick a name and save your 12-word recovery phrase. It's generated on your device and never sent anywhere.

  2. Invite with a QR code

    Show a QR code or share an invite code. Nobody joins without an existing member approving them.

  3. Share, on your terms

    Everyone sees each other on the map. Pause sharing or leave the family whenever you want.

What's inside

Everything a family needs, nothing it doesn't.

Live family map

See everyone in real time, review the last 30 days of movement, and get a drive-time estimate when you ask for one.

Place and speed alerts

Know when someone arrives at school or leaves home, and when a driver goes faster than you've set.

Crash detection

Optional. If a likely crash is detected and the driver doesn't answer within a minute, the family is alerted.

Private chat and files

Family messages and shared files, sealed end to end. Only your family can open them.

Encrypted vault

Keep important documents in a passphrase-protected vault that sits inside the app.

Works on home Wi-Fi

Phones on the same network can talk directly to each other, which also means faster updates at home.

Private by design

You don't have to take our word for it.

Locations, messages and files are encrypted on your phone with keys that never leave it, using libsodium (X25519, XSalsa20-Poly1305, Ed25519). A relay server passes the sealed envelopes along. It can't open them, and neither can we.

What only your family can see

  • Where you are, and your location history
  • Your messages and shared files
  • Your names, photos and vault documents
  • Who's online

What the relay can see

  • That a device connected, and when
  • Pseudonymous member IDs
  • Roughly how often each device sends something. Messages are padded so their length reveals nothing.
Two features do reach outside services. The map loads its tiles from OpenStreetMap, and if you tap a drive-time estimate, the two locations involved are sent to the public OSRM routing service. Nothing else leaves the encrypted channel. Both are spelled out in the privacy policy.
