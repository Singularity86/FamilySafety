Family Safety

Know where your family is. Without anyone else knowing.

Family Safety is a private location-sharing and messaging app for families. Everything is end-to-end encrypted between your family's own devices — there are no accounts, no ads, and no company server that can read your locations or messages.

What it does

Live family map

See every family member's location in real time, with location history and drive-time estimates.

Private messaging

Family chat and one-to-one messages, encrypted end to end with delivery and read receipts.

Safety alerts

Place arrival and departure alerts, and optional vehicle crash detection that notifies your family.

No accounts

No email, phone number, or password. Your identity is a recovery phrase generated on your own device.

Family-run backup

History is stored encrypted on your family's own devices and replicated between them — no cloud copy.

Works on home Wi-Fi

Devices on the same network talk directly to each other, no internet required.

Why you don't have to trust us

Locations and messages are encrypted on your phone with keys that never leave it (libsodium: X25519, XSalsa20-Poly1305, Ed25519). The relay server that forwards messages between devices sees only sealed envelopes it cannot open — and neither can we.

Don't take our word for it: the source code is public. Read it, build it yourself, or point out something we got wrong.
